# Changelog

Changes to the [password generator](https://maxxthunder.github.io/rocknrollracing/). The version is shown at the bottom of the page.

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-17)
- Planet pictures on the planet picker, which now matches the character picker.
- Spinning cars on the vehicle picker, ripped from the SNES car shop in all five paints; the picker lists cars cheapest first and the Color names are spelled out.
- Engine, tires, shocks, armor and weapon icons next to their fields.
- A full Secret codes section with Super Nintendo, Genesis and Game Boy Advance tabs. Every code was checked in an emulator.
- The shopkeeper is named Fast Eddie, as the game calls him.
- Google Analytics.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-17)
- Game logo in the header and racer portraits on the character picker.
- New "The cast" section with every rival, named as the ROM spells them.
- Notes on the Japanese, European and Game Boy Advance releases, including the GBA button codes and its own Advance a Level code.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-16)
- First release: make or decode Rock N' Roll Racing passwords for Super Nintendo and Genesis.
- Glitch mode for out-of-range values, Big text view, copy button, and suggested fixes for mistyped passwords.
- Notes on the secret passwords and button codes found in both ROMs.
