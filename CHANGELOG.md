# Changelog

Changes to the [password generator](https://maxxthunder.github.io/rocknrollracing/). The version is shown at the bottom of the page.

## 1.5.1 (2026-10-30)
- Big text has a typing guide: the game's password grid with the next character lit and the moves to reach it.
- Undo for a few seconds after Max everything, New game or loading a password (Z on a computer).
- After loading a password, the characters that change are underlined, so only those need retyping.
- Point at a password character to see which settings it holds, or at a setting to light up its characters.

## 1.5.0 (2026-10-27)
- A password decodes as soon as all 12 characters are typed or pasted.
- Keyboard shortcuts on a computer: paste a password anywhere on the page to decode it, C copies, B opens Big text.
- Money has − and + buttons (also on each digit in glitch mode, which stack on phones), and the box can be cleared to type a new amount without snapping back to 0.
- On phones, the weapon sliders follow your finger anywhere along them without scrolling the page, and autocorrect is off in the decode box.
- Bigger tap targets for the Glitch mode switch and the section headings at the bottom.

## 1.4.3 (2026-10-17)
- README: the European Super Nintendo beta, whose opening is marked "For Ocean's customers only" and adds a Software Creations logo.

## 1.4.2 (2026-10-17)
- Corrected the README: Beta 1's "Commando" isn't a cut car. It's the Dirt Devil under an earlier name, and the names after it are shifted one car along.

## 1.4.1 (2026-10-17)
- Snake Sanders is now the browser tab icon.

## 1.4.0 (2026-10-17)
- New "Beta builds" section in the README: two US Super Nintendo prototypes, including Beta 1's Commando (the Dirt Devil's earlier name), its "Rock & Roll Racing" title and other cut text, with screenshots.

## 1.3.1 (2026-10-17)
- The Difficulty, Division, Color and upgrade options now split their row evenly instead of bunching up on the left.

## 1.3.0 (2026-10-17)
- Glitch colors 5–7 show on the spinning cars as the two-tone paints the game really draws: teal and purple, orange and green, green and dark green.
- New codes now wear a "★ New" tab on their card.
- README rewritten: car gallery, code tables for every console, what each glitch value does, and where else the ROMs were searched for secrets.
- MIT license.

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-17)
- Planet pictures on the planet picker, which now matches the character picker.
- Spinning cars on the vehicle picker, ripped from the SNES car shop in all five paints; the picker lists cars cheapest first and the Color names are spelled out.
- Engine, tires, shocks, armor and weapon icons next to their fields.
- A full Secret codes section with Super Nintendo, Genesis and Game Boy Advance tabs. Every code was checked in an emulator.
- The shopkeeper is named Fast Eddie, as the game calls him.
- Google Analytics.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-17)
- Game logo in the header and racer portraits on the character picker.
- New "The cast" section with every rival, named as the ROM spells them.
- Notes on the Japanese, European and Game Boy Advance releases, including the GBA button codes and its own Advance a Level code.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-16)
- First release: make or decode Rock N' Roll Racing passwords for Super Nintendo and Genesis.
- Glitch mode for out-of-range values, Big text view, copy button, and suggested fixes for mistyped passwords.
- Notes on the secret passwords and button codes found in both ROMs.
